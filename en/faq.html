Frequently asked questions

Search answers or open a question.

Is Signal UA official?

No. Follow official emergency alerts and civil protection instructions.

Why are notifications delayed?

Data sources, internet connectivity and Android background restrictions may cause delays.

How do I enable notifications?

Allow notifications in Android, choose regions and check sound and vibration settings.

What is elevated danger?

It is an additional location-specific threat signal, not the same as an ordinary air-raid alert.

Why is the map not updating?

Check your internet connection. External providers may be temporarily unavailable.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the main features.

Does the app use location?

Optional location access can be used when you grant Android permission.

How do I delete settings?

Use Android Settings → Apps → Signal UA → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall.

Can I rely only on Signal UA?

No. Follow official warnings and instructions.
