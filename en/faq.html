Frequently asked questions

Search answers or open a question.

Is Signal UA official?

No. Follow official emergency alerts and civil protection instructions.

Why are notifications delayed?

Data sources, internet connectivity and Android background restrictions may cause delays.

How do I enable notifications?

Allow notifications in Android, choose regions and check sound and vibration settings.

What is elevated danger?

It is an additional location-specific threat signal, not the same as an ordinary air-raid alert.

Why is the map not updating?

Check your internet connection. External providers may be temporarily unavailable.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the main features.

Does the app use location?

Optional location access can be used when you grant Android permission.

How do I delete settings?

Use Android Settings → Apps → Signal UA → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall.

Can I rely only on Signal UA?

No. Follow official warnings and instructions.

How do I contact the developer?

Email app.trivoga@gmail.com.

How can I test notifications?

Settings → Test region. Add it for testing and remove it afterward. Test messages are not real alerts.

Does monitoring work after reboot?

The app attempts to restore enabled monitoring. Android may restrict startup. Open Signal UA after reboot and check its status.

Why is monitoring waiting to start?

The setting is enabled but service operation is not confirmed. Check internet, permissions and battery restrictions. Do not rely solely on the app.

What happens after Force stop?

Android may prevent background alerts until the app is reopened. Open Signal UA and check monitoring.

What happens without internet?

New information may not arrive. Displayed status or history may be stale. No message does not mean all-clear.

Is satellite imagery live?

No. It is Esri basemap imagery, not live footage. Alerts and threats are supplied separately.

Why are there no markers during an alert?

Alert status and NEPTUN markers are separate data. No marker does not mean no danger. Marker positions and movement may be estimated.

How do I add a widget?

Long-press an empty area of the Android home screen → Widgets → Signal UA. Selected-area, large map and small map widgets are available.

How do I delete local settings?

Android Settings → Apps → Signal UA → Storage → Clear data. Configure alerts again afterward. This does not delete external server logs.

What if the data differs from official alerts?

Follow official warnings for your area. Report the region, time, app version and discrepancy to the developer.
