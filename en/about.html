About Signal UA

Signal UA is an independent information app, not a government emergency alert system.

What does it do?

Shows available air-raid alerts, event history and map information for selected territories in Ukraine.

Alerts and elevated danger

Elevated danger requires an additional location-specific threat signal. A regular air-raid alert does not automatically trigger it.

Map and data sources

Alerts are retrieved through a Cloudflare Worker endpoint; aerial threat information is requested from NEPTUN. The app also uses local administrative geometry data. External data can be delayed or unavailable.

Notifications

Local notifications, sound, vibration and background monitoring depend on Android permissions, device restrictions and connectivity.

Location

Optional device location can help determine the user's area when permission is granted.
