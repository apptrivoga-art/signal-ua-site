About Signal UA

Updated: November 24, 2026

Independent service

Signal UA is not an app of government authorities or the State Emergency Service and does not replace official alerts. No notification does not mean safety. Follow official instructions.

Features

Alert status, selected oblasts, districts and communities, history, maps and available threat reports. Background monitoring and notifications are enabled by default. Voice alerts, vibration, a simulated test region and three widget options are available.

Getting started

Add areas in the Alert tab using “+” next to My status. Allow Android notifications, check sound and monitoring, and verify the service after reboot. See FAQ and user help.

Air-raid alerts and history

Data from alerts.in.ua arrives through the Signal UA API on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare is infrastructure, not the primary alert source.

Threat information

NEPTUN provides separate unofficial reports and markers. Positions and movement may be estimated, incomplete or stale. No marker does not mean no danger.

Maps

OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap contributors provide the street basemap; Esri World Imagery and credited providers supply satellite imagery, not live footage. Local geographic boundaries do not determine alert status.
