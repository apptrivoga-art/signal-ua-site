Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026.

Signal UA is an independent information app operated by an individual developer. Contact: app.trivoga@gmail.com.

Information processed

Selected regions, alert and sound settings may be stored locally using SharedPreferences. With your permission, the app can access approximate or precise device location to determine an area. Notifications and background services use Android permissions.

Network requests

Alert data is retrieved from a Cloudflare Worker endpoint; threat data is retrieved from NEPTUN. The providers may process IP addresses, request times and technical connection data according to their own practices. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process website access logs.

Purpose and legal basis

Information is used to provide alerts, map features, selected-area information, local settings and technical operation. Processing is based on applicable legal grounds, including consent where required and providing requested app functions.

Sharing and international processing

Technical requests may reach third-party service providers, including providers located outside Ukraine. The developer does not claim that all external network metadata is stored exclusively on your device.

Retention and deletion

Local settings remain until cleared or the app is uninstalled. External providers set their own log retention periods. Contact emails may be retained as needed to address your request and meet legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

Manage location and notification permissions in Android settings. To request access, correction, deletion or object to processing where applicable, email app.trivoga@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or seek judicial protection.

Security and changes

Reasonable technical safeguards are used; no internet service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated when app functions or data practices change.

Relevant law: Law of Ukraine on Personal Data Protection.
