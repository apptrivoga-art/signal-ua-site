Privacy policy

Updated: November 24, 2026

1. Responsible developer

Signal UA is an independent information app operated by an individual developer, not a government service. Privacy contact: app.trivoga@gmail.com. This policy covers the 1.239 branch and separately notes older implementations.

2. Device data

Selected areas, language, map, sound, vibration, notification and monitoring settings, cached data and operational settings are stored locally. The main features do not require an account, name, phone number or email. No advertising or analytics SDK was identified in the reviewed mobile code.

3. Location

With Android permission, approximate or precise coordinates may be accessed for area selection and danger assessment. Coordinates are matched with geographic data on the device. Last known coordinates may be stored locally and used by background monitoring. No direct transmission of these coordinates to the alert API was identified in the reviewed code. Map tile requests may reveal the viewed area, including when centered on your location. You may revoke permission in Android and select areas manually.

4. Network providers

Alert status and history use the Signal UA API on Cloudflare Workers (alerts.in.ua data). Threat information uses HTTPS and WSS from NEPTUN. Requests may include selected area identifiers, IP addresses, timestamps, headers and other network metadata. Maps contact OpenFreeMap and Esri; providers can see requested tiles. Older implementations included tryvoha.online, CARTO, unpkg and GitHub Raw; this is not a list of active 1.239 sources. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Providers may process technical data under their own policies, including outside Ukraine.

5. Permissions and monitoring

Internet and network status support updates; notifications, sound and vibration support alerts. Background monitoring and notifications are on by default and user-controlled. The service uses background operation, wake locks and restart after reboot. Battery optimization exemption is voluntary. Location and notification display depend on Android permissions. Disabling monitoring or permissions can stop alerts.

6. Support and external links

If you email app.trivoga@gmail.com, your sender address, message and attachments are processed to answer and resolve your inquiry. External websites and voluntary support through monobank have their own data practices. The app does not collect payment details in its own form.

7. Purposes and legal grounds

Processing supports requested features, alerts, maps, technical operation and responses to inquiries. Depending on the operation, consent, provision of a requested feature or another lawful ground applies. The app is not designed to deliberately collect children's personal data.

8. Retention and deletion

Local settings and cache remain until updated, cleared or uninstalled. Use Android Settings → Apps → Signal UA → Storage → Clear data. This resets settings; configure alerts again afterward. Support correspondence is retained as reasonably necessary to resolve inquiries and meet legal obligations. Technical log retention depends on hosting configuration and providers; clearing device data does not delete such logs. To request deletion of data held by the developer, email app.trivoga@gmail.com. There is no separate account deletion process because the app does not create accounts.

9. Security

Main network connections use HTTPS and WSS. Local settings are stored on the device. Absolute security is not guaranteed. Avoid sending passwords, documents or precise coordinates to support unnecessarily.

10. Rights and changes

You can manage permissions, change areas, clear local data and request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent where applicable. Rights include those under Ukraine's Law on Personal Data Protection. You may contact the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights. This page is updated when data practices change.

Signal UA is not an official alert system. Contact: app.trivoga@gmail.com
