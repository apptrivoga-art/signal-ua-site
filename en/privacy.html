Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026.

Signal UA is an independent information app operated by an individual developer. Contact: app.trivoga@gmail.com.

Information processed

Selected regions, alert and sound settings may be stored locally using SharedPreferences. With your permission, the app can access approximate or precise device location to determine an area. Notifications and background services use Android permissions.

Network requests

Alert data is retrieved from a Cloudflare Worker endpoint; threat data is retrieved from NEPTUN. The providers may process IP addresses, request times and technical connection data according to their own practices. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process website access logs.

Purpose and legal basis

Information is used to provide alerts, map features, selected-area information, local settings and technical operation. Processing is based on applicable legal grounds, including consent where required and providing requested app functions.

Sharing and international processing

Technical requests may reach third-party service providers, including providers located outside Ukraine. The developer does not claim that all external network metadata is stored exclusively on your device.

Retention and deletion

Local settings remain until cleared or the app is uninstalled. External providers set their own log retention periods. Contact emails may be retained as needed to address your request and meet legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

Manage location and notification permissions in Android settings. To request access, correction, deletion or object to processing where applicable, email app.trivoga@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or seek judicial protection.

Security and changes

Reasonable technical safeguards are used; no internet service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated when app functions or data practices change.

Relevant law: Law of Ukraine on Personal Data Protection.

Who is responsible

Signal UA is maintained by an independent individual developer, not a government agency. Privacy contact: app.trivoga@gmail.com.

Data and purposes

Selected regions, districts or communities and sound, vibration, notification and background settings are stored locally. With Android permission, the app may access precise or approximate location to identify the current community. In the reviewed location function, coordinates are matched against community geometry on the device; no upload of coordinates was found in that function. You can deny location access and select a region manually.

The app requests alert statuses, history, threat information and map resources from external servers. These servers may receive IP addresses, request times, HTTP headers and requested resource paths. Depending on circumstances, these may constitute personal data.

Normal use requires no account, name, telephone number or email address. If you email the developer, your email address, message and attachments are processed to respond and resolve your inquiry.

Third-party services

Services identified in reviewed code include Cloudflare Workers (alert API), NEPTUN (threat data), tryvoha.online (events and regions), CARTO/OpenStreetMap (maps), unpkg (Leaflet resources) and GitHub Raw (geodata). External sites, including optional monobank support, are accessed when you open the respective link. These providers may process technical data and retain their own logs under their policies; Signal UA does not control their retention periods.

Permissions and background operation

Android may request precise/approximate location and notification permissions. The app also uses internet, foreground service, vibration, wake lock, boot-completed and battery optimization exemption request permissions to support alerts and background updates. Notifications can be delayed or unavailable. You can change permissions in Android settings.

Retention and legal basis

Local preferences remain until changed, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Location use depends on Android permission. Voluntary correspondence is processed to respond and resolve requests and retained only as reasonably necessary for those purposes and applicable legal obligations. Independent providers determine retention of their server logs.

Security and international processing

The app uses HTTPS endpoints where configured. No internet transmission is guaranteed completely secure. External service providers may process technical request data outside Ukraine. We do not sell user personal data; technical processing by service providers is not a sale.

Your rights and deletion

You may revoke permissions, change selected areas, clear app data or uninstall the app. This removes local settings but does not guarantee deletion of independent providers' logs. For access, correction or deletion requests concerning data handled by the developer (including correspondence), contact app.trivoga@gmail.com. The current app does not create user accounts, so account deletion is not applicable. Rights are governed in part by the Ukrainian Personal Data Protection Law.

Children, updates and safety

The app is not intended to intentionally collect children's personal data. This policy will be updated if app functionality, service providers or processing practices change. Signal UA is not an official warning system. Follow official civil protection alerts and instructions in emergencies.
